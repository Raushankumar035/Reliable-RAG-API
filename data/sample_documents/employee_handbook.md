# Helio Systems Employee Handbook

Helio Systems is a software company headquartered in Bengaluru, India. This handbook summarises the main policies that apply to all full-time employees.

## Leave Policy

Every full-time employee receives 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Annual leave accrues monthly at 2 days per month. Employees may carry forward a maximum of 5 unused annual leave days into the next calendar year; any balance above 5 days expires on 31 December.

Employees receive 10 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is required for sick leave longer than 2 consecutive days. Sick leave cannot be carried forward.

Parental leave: the primary caregiver receives 16 weeks of fully paid leave, and the secondary caregiver receives 6 weeks of fully paid leave. Parental leave must be started within 12 months of the child's birth or adoption.

## Remote Work

Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week, in agreement with their team lead. Everyone is expected to be available during core hours, which are 11:00 to 16:00 India Standard Time (IST), on all working days. Fully remote arrangements need written approval from the department head.

## Travel and Expenses

When travelling for work, employees receive a meal allowance of INR 500 per day. Any single expense above INR 10,000 requires prior approval from the employee's manager. Expense claims must be submitted within 30 days of the expense date, together with receipts; late claims are rejected unless Finance grants an exception.

## Employment Terms

New employees serve a probation period of 3 months. After confirmation, the notice period for resignation is 60 days. During probation the notice period is 15 days.
