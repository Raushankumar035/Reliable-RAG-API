# Helio Insights - Product FAQ

Helio Insights is Helio Systems' cloud analytics product for tracking business metrics.

## Plans and Pricing

Helio Insights is offered in three plans:

- **Starter**: USD 29 per user per month, limited to a maximum of 5 users.
- **Growth**: USD 79 per user per month, up to 50 users.
- **Enterprise**: custom pricing, unlimited users.

All plans include a free trial of 14 days. No credit card is required to start the trial.

## Data Retention

Data retention depends on the plan. Starter keeps data for 90 days. Growth keeps data for 1 year. Enterprise retention is configurable up to 7 years.

## Support

Starter customers get email support only. Growth customers get email and chat support, available 24 hours a day on 5 days a week (24x5). Enterprise customers get 24x7 phone support and a dedicated customer success manager.

## Uptime SLA

Growth plans are covered by a 99.9% uptime SLA. Enterprise plans are covered by a 99.99% uptime SLA. The Starter plan has no uptime SLA.

## Single Sign-On (SSO)

SAML-based single sign-on (SSO) is available on the Growth and Enterprise plans only. It is not available on Starter.

## API Rate Limits

The REST API is rate limited per plan: Starter allows 60 requests per minute, Growth allows 600 requests per minute, and Enterprise allows 6,000 requests per minute.
